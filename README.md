# 📊 E-Commerce Sales Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to track overall e-commerce performance, profit margins, shipping modes, and regional sales distribution to empower data-driven business decisions.

---

## ❓ Problem Statement
E-commerce decision-makers face challenges in identifying profit leaks, monitoring high-cost shipping methods, and understanding regional customer behavior. Without consolidated visual tracking, it becomes difficult to pinpoint loss-making products and optimize checkout payment options.

---

## 🎯 Goal of the Dashboard
The goal of this Power BI report is to provide real-time visual insights to:
* Track primary performance KPIs (Total Orders, Revenue, Net Profit, Average Order Value).
* Identify top-selling sub-categories vs. bottom loss-making products.
* Analyze customer preferences regarding payment methods, segments, and shipping modes.
* Compare year-over-year monthly trends for sales and profitability (2019 vs 2020).

---

## 📊 Key Dashboard Metrics (KPIs)
* **Orders:** 22K
* **Total Sales:** $1.57M
* **Total Profit:** $175K
* **Average Order Value (AOV):** $70

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack Used
* **Power BI Desktop:** Core dashboard visualization and layout design.
* **Power Query:** Data transformation, dynamic data cleaning, and custom columns.
* **DAX Formulas:** Created custom measures for total sales, net profit, order counts, and YoY time-intelligence calculations.
* **Data Modeling:** Established star-schema relationships between transaction records and lookup attributes.

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights (From Dashboard)
* **Payment Preference:** Cash on Delivery (COD) leads sales at **$667.42K**, closely followed by Online Payments (**$553.59K**).
* **Regional & Segment Lead:** The **West Region ($522.33K)** is the highest revenue generator, and the **Consumer Segment ($730K+)** drives the bulk of total orders.
* **Shipment Method:** **Standard Class** is the most preferred shipping mode (**$912.4K**), indicating customers prefer lower shipping costs over same-day delivery.
* **Profit Bleed Alert:** The **Bottom 5 Products by Profit** visual highlights items causing significant net losses (e.g., Cubify products with negative margins up to **-$6.2K**), requiring urgent pricing adjustments.
